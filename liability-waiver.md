## 1. Acknowledgment of Risk

### 1.1 Inherent Dangers of Skateboarding
You acknowledge and understand that **skateboarding is an inherently dangerous activity** that involves significant risks of serious injury or death. These risks exist regardless of the care, caution, instruction, or expertise of the participants.

### 1.2 Types of Risks
Skateboarding involves risks including, but not limited to:
- **Physical injuries:** Cuts, bruises, scrapes, sprains, strains, fractures, broken bones, dislocations
- **Head and brain injuries:** Concussions, traumatic brain injury, skull fractures
- **Spinal injuries:** Back injuries, neck injuries, paralysis, permanent disability
- **Internal injuries:** Organ damage, internal bleeding
- **Dental injuries:** Broken or lost teeth, jaw injuries
- **Other serious injuries:** Loss of consciousness, permanent scarring, disfigurement
- **Death:** Skateboarding can result in fatal injuries

### 1.3 Environmental Risks
Additional risks include:
- Collisions with other skaters, pedestrians, vehicles, or objects
- Falls due to uneven surfaces, obstacles, or equipment failure
- Injuries caused by skateboard equipment malfunction or defects
- Weather conditions (rain, ice, heat) affecting safety
- Inadequate lighting or visibility
- Performing tricks on stairs, rails, ramps, or other obstacles

### 1.4 Tricks Generated by the App
You understand that:
- Skate Monkey generates skateboarding tricks ranging from basic to advanced difficulty
- **The App does not assess your skill level or physical ability**
- **The App does not determine whether a trick is safe or appropriate for you**
- Tricks may be beyond your current skill level and attempting them could result in serious injury
- You are solely responsible for determining whether to attempt any trick

### 1.5 No Instruction or Supervision
Skate Monkey is **not** a skateboarding instruction app. We do not:
- Teach you how to skateboard
- Provide coaching or guidance on trick execution
- Supervise your skateboarding activities
- Assess the safety of your environment
- Provide warnings about specific trick difficulty

## 2. Assumption of Risk

### 2.1 Voluntary Participation
You voluntarily choose to use Skate Monkey and participate in skateboarding activities. You are not required to attempt any trick generated by the App.

**You are not required to attempt every trick.** You may mark any trick as "missed" without attempting it if you deem it too difficult, dangerous, or beyond your skill level. **Your safety is more important than winning the game.** Do not risk serious injury to avoid receiving a letter or losing a game.

### 2.2 Full Assumption of All Risks
**BY USING SKATE MONKEY, YOU VOLUNTARILY ASSUME ALL RISKS** associated with skateboarding, including but not limited to the risks described in Section 1.

You expressly assume the risk of:
- Falls, collisions, and equipment failure
- Serious injury, permanent disability, or death
- Property damage to your skateboard or other belongings
- Injuries resulting from your own negligence or the negligence of others
- Injuries occurring whether or not you are attempting a trick from the App

### 2.3 Safety Recommendations

**WE STRONGLY RECOMMEND THAT YOU:**

#### Essential Safety Gear
- **Always wear a helmet** designed for skateboarding or similar action sports
- **Wear knee pads** to protect against knee injuries
- **Wear elbow pads** to protect against elbow and arm injuries
- **Wear wrist guards** to protect against wrist fractures and injuries
- Wear appropriate footwear designed for skateboarding

#### Safe Practices
- **Only attempt tricks within your skill level**
- Practice new tricks in a safe, controlled environment before attempting them for the App
- **Skate only in areas where skateboarding is legal and permitted**
- Obtain permission before skating on private property
- **Respect public and private property** - you are responsible for any damage you cause to rails, ledges, benches, or other structures
- Avoid skating in areas with heavy traffic, pedestrians, or other hazards
- Do not trespass on private property
- **Inspect your skateboard before each session:** Check wheels, trucks, deck, and hardware for damage or wear
- Warm up and stretch before skateboarding
- Stay hydrated and take breaks as needed
- Stop immediately if you feel pain, fatigue, or discomfort
- **Never skateboard under the influence of alcohol, drugs, or medications that impair judgment or coordination**

#### Physical Readiness
- Consult with a physician before beginning skateboarding if you have any medical conditions, injuries, or physical limitations
- Do not skateboard if you are injured, ill, or physically impaired
- Ensure you are well-rested and physically prepared

#### Environmental Awareness
- Skate in well-lit areas with good visibility
- Avoid skating in wet, icy, or otherwise hazardous conditions
- Be aware of your surroundings and watch for obstacles, pedestrians, and vehicles
- Do not skate in prohibited areas

### 2.4 Your Responsibility
**YOU ACKNOWLEDGE THAT YOU ARE SOLELY RESPONSIBLE FOR:**
- Your decision to use Skate Monkey and skateboard
- Determining your own skill level and physical limitations
- Deciding whether to attempt any trick
- Assessing the safety of your skateboard and equipment
- Evaluating the safety of your skating environment
- Using appropriate safety gear
- Seeking proper instruction and training
- Following all applicable laws and regulations

## 6. Video Recording and Submission

### 6.1 Safety While Filming
When recording videos for Skate Monkey, you acknowledge additional risks including:
- Distraction from filming or camera operation
- Attempting tricks specifically for video recording purposes
- Performing in front of a camera, which may increase pressure or risk-taking behavior

### 6.2 Your Responsibility While Filming
When filming trick attempts, you are responsible for:
- **Wearing appropriate safety gear** (helmet, pads, guards)
- Ensuring the filming location is safe and appropriate
- Not attempting tricks beyond your skill level just to capture video
- Ensuring that filming does not create additional hazards
- The safety of anyone assisting you with filming

### 6.3 Filming Environment
You should:
- Film in safe, legal locations appropriate for skateboarding
- Ensure adequate lighting and visibility
- Avoid filming in areas with pedestrians, traffic, or other hazards
- Not trespass or film in prohibited areas

### 6.4 Video Requirements
To mark a trick as successfully landed, you **must** record and upload a video of your attempt. If you choose not to film a trick attempt, you can only mark the trick as "missed" and will receive a letter. You are not obligated to film every trick, but filming is required to receive credit for landing a trick.
