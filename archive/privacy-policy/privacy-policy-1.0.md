## 1. Introduction

Welcome to Skate Monkey! This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, share, and protect your personal information when you use the Skate Monkey mobile application (the "App" or "Service").

**Who We Are:**
Skate Monkey LLC is a multiplayer skateboarding game app that allows users to compete with friends, record trick attempts, and communicate through in-app messaging.

**Scope of This Policy:**
This Privacy Policy applies to all users of the Skate Monkey mobile application. By using our Service, you agree to the collection and use of information as described in this Privacy Policy.

**Important:** Please also review our [Terms and Conditions](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/skatemonkeydev/skate-monkey-legal/main/terms-and-conditions.md) and [Liability Waiver](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/skatemonkeydev/skate-monkey-legal/main/liability-waiver.md).

## 2. Information We Collect

We collect several types of information from and about users of our App.

### 2.1 Account Information
When you create an account, we collect:
- **Email address:** Used for account creation, authentication, and communications
- **Username:** Your unique identifier (3-20 characters, lowercase alphanumeric and underscores)
- **Display name:** The name shown to other users
- **Password:** Securely hashed and managed by Firebase Authentication (we do not have access to plain-text passwords)

### 2.2 Profile Information
- **Profile photo:** Optional image you upload to personalize your profile
- **User preferences:** Game settings and app preferences

### 2.3 Authentication Data
If you choose to sign in using Google Sign-In:
- **Google account information:** Name, email address, and profile picture from your Google account
- **Google ID token:** Used to authenticate your identity

### 2.4 Device Information
We automatically collect:
- **Device type and model:** (e.g., iPhone 14, Samsung Galaxy S23)
- **Operating system and version:** (e.g., iOS 17, Android 13)
- **Firebase Cloud Messaging (FCM) token:** Unique identifier for sending push notifications to your device
- **App version:** The version of Skate Monkey you're using

### 2.5 Usage Data
We collect information about how you use the App:
- **Game activity:** Games created, players invited, tricks generated, game results
- **Trick submissions:** Records of tricks you attempted and whether you landed or missed them
- **App interactions:** Features you use, screens you view, buttons you tap

### 2.6 User-Generated Content
- **Videos:** Skateboarding trick videos you record and upload
- **Messages:** Text messages you send to other players in your games
- **Video messages:** Videos you send as messages to other players

### 2.7 Technical Data
- **IP address:** Your device's internet protocol address
- **Crash logs and error reports:** Technical information when the App crashes or encounters errors
- **Performance data:** App loading times and performance metrics

## 6. Data Storage and Security

### 6.1 Where Your Data Is Stored
Your data is stored using Firebase services operated by Google Cloud:
- **Data centers:** United States
- **Firestore database:** Cloud-hosted NoSQL database
- **Firebase Storage:** Cloud storage for videos
- **Encryption in transit:** All data transmitted between your device and our servers is encrypted using HTTPS/TLS

### 6.2 Security Measures
We implement industry-standard security measures to protect your data:
- **Password security:** Firebase Authentication securely hashes passwords (passwords are never stored in plain text)
- **Firebase security rules:** Database and storage access restricted to authenticated users
- **HTTPS/TLS encryption:** All data transmissions are encrypted
- **Regular security updates:** We keep our systems updated with security patches

### 6.3 No Guarantee of Absolute Security
While we take reasonable measures to protect your data, no internet transmission or electronic storage is 100% secure. We cannot guarantee absolute security of your information.

### 6.4 Your Responsibility
You are responsible for:
- Keeping your password confidential
- Not sharing your account credentials
- Logging out on shared devices
- Notifying us immediately of any unauthorized access

## 7. Data Retention

### 7.1 Active Accounts
We retain your information for as long as your account is active or as needed to provide you the Service.

### 7.2 Game Data and Content
**To manage storage costs, we may automatically delete inactive or completed games and their associated content (videos, messages) after a period of inactivity.** This helps us keep the Service affordable and sustainable. **You should keep your original videos** as we do not guarantee indefinite storage of game content.

### 7.3 Account Deletion
When you delete your account:
- Your account information, videos, and messages will be deleted within **30-90 days**
- Some data may be retained in backup systems for up to an additional 90 days
- We may retain certain information as required by law or for legitimate business purposes (e.g., to prevent fraud, resolve disputes, enforce our Terms)

### 7.4 Legal Obligations
We may retain information longer if required by:
- Legal obligations (e.g., tax records, court orders)
- Ongoing investigations
- Dispute resolution

### 7.5 Anonymized Data
We may retain anonymized or aggregated data (data that cannot identify you) indefinitely for analytics and service improvement.

## 8. Your Rights and Choices

You have the following rights regarding your personal information:

### 8.1 Access Your Data
You can view and access your profile information through the App at any time.

**Request a data export:** You may request a copy of all personal data we have about you by emailing skatemonkeydev@gmail.com with the subject "Data Export Request." We will respond within **30-45 days** with your data in a portable format.

### 8.2 Correct Your Data
You can update your profile information (display name, username, profile photo) directly in the App settings.

For corrections to other data, contact us at skatemonkeydev@gmail.com.

### 8.3 Delete Your Data
**Delete your account:** You may request account deletion by emailing skatemonkeydev@gmail.com with the subject "Account Deletion Request."

Upon deletion, your data will be removed within 30-90 days as described in Section 7.

### 8.4 Opt-Out of Push Notifications
You can disable push notifications at any time through:
- **iOS:** Settings > Notifications > Skate Monkey
- **Android:** Settings > Apps > Skate Monkey > Notifications

Note: Disabling notifications may impact your ability to stay updated on game activity.

### 8.5 Opt-Out of Marketing Communications
If we offer marketing communications in the future, you may opt out by:
- Clicking "unsubscribe" in the email
- Emailing skatemonkeydev@gmail.com

You cannot opt out of essential service communications (e.g., account security alerts, Terms updates).

## 9. Children's Privacy (Ages 13+)

### 9.1 Age Requirement
Skate Monkey is intended for users **ages 13 and older**. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

### 9.2 Compliance with COPPA
In compliance with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA):
- We require users to be at least 13 years old
- We do not knowingly collect information from children under 13
- If we discover a user is under 13, we will immediately terminate the account and delete all associated data

### 9.3 Notice for Parents and Legal Guardians of Users Ages 13-17
**Parents and legal guardians of users ages 13-17 should be aware:**
- Minors may create accounts with permission from a parent or legal guardian
- Minors' usernames, display names, profile photos, videos, and messages will be visible to other players in their games
- Minors may participate in games with adult users (18+)
- Minors may send and receive messages from other players, including adults
- We do not actively monitor all communications
- Parents and legal guardians should discuss online safety and appropriate behavior with their children
- Parents and legal guardians are encouraged to supervise their children's use of the App

### 9.4 If You Believe a Child Under 13 Is Using the App
If you believe a child under 13 has created an account, please contact us immediately at skatemonkeydev@gmail.com. We will investigate and take appropriate action, including account termination and data deletion.

## 12. California Privacy Rights (CCPA)

If you are a California resident, you have additional rights under the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA).

### 12.1 Right to Know
You have the right to request:
- Categories of personal information we collect
- Categories of sources from which we collect information
- Business or commercial purposes for collecting information
- Categories of third parties with whom we share information
- Specific pieces of personal information we have about you

### 12.2 Right to Delete
You have the right to request deletion of your personal information, subject to certain exceptions (e.g., legal obligations, ongoing transactions, security purposes).

### 12.3 Right to Opt-Out of Sale
**We do not sell your personal information.** Therefore, there is no need to opt out of sales.

### 12.4 Right to Non-Discrimination
You have the right not to receive discriminatory treatment for exercising your CCPA rights. We will not:
- Deny you goods or services
- Charge different prices or rates
- Provide a different level of quality
- Suggest you will receive a different price or quality

### 12.5 How to Exercise Your Rights
To exercise your CCPA rights, contact us at:
- **Email:** skatemonkeydev@gmail.com
- **Subject Line:** "California Privacy Rights Request"

Include:
- Your full name
- Email address associated with your account
- Description of the information you're requesting

We will respond within **45 days** (may be extended by an additional 45 days if necessary).

### 12.6 Verification
To protect your privacy, we will verify your identity before fulfilling your request. We may ask you to:
- Confirm your email address
- Provide account information
- Answer security questions

### 12.7 Authorized Agents
You may designate an authorized agent to make requests on your behalf. The agent must provide proof of authorization, and we may still require you to verify your identity.
