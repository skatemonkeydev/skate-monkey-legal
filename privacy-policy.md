## 1. Introduction

Welcome to Skate Monkey! This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, share, and protect your personal information when you use the Skate Monkey mobile application (the "App" or "Service").

**Who We Are:**
Skate Monkey LLC is a multiplayer skateboarding game app that allows users to compete with friends, record trick attempts, and communicate through in-app messaging.

**Scope of This Policy:**
This Privacy Policy applies to all users of the Skate Monkey mobile application. By using our Service, you agree to the collection and use of information as described in this Privacy Policy.

**Important:** Please also review our [Terms and Conditions](https://skatemonkey.app/terms) and [Liability Waiver](https://skatemonkey.app/waiver).

## 2. Information We Collect

We collect several types of information from and about users of our App.

### 2.1 Account Information
When you create an account, we collect:
- **Email address:** Used for account creation, authentication, and communications
- **Username:** Your unique identifier (3-20 characters, lowercase alphanumeric and underscores)
- **Display name:** The name shown to other users
- **Password:** Securely hashed and managed by Firebase Authentication (we do not have access to plain-text passwords)

### 2.2 Profile Information
- **Profile photo:** Optional image you upload to personalize your profile. We do not use profile photos for automatic recognition or extract facial geometry or other identifiers from them to identify or verify individuals.
- **Skill level:** Your skateboarding skill level recorded in your profile
- **Footedness:** Your skateboarding stance recorded in your profile
- **User preferences:** Game settings and app preferences
- **Date of birth:** Entered on the "complete-your-profile" screen after sign-in to determine whether you meet the App's age requirement
- **Policy acceptance:** The latest legal-document version you have accepted is recorded in your user account

### 2.3 Authentication Data
If you choose to sign in using Google Sign-In:
- **Google account information:** Name, email address, and profile picture from your Google account
- **Google ID token:** Used to authenticate your identity

### 2.4 Device Information
We automatically collect:
- **Device type and model:** (e.g., iPhone 14, Samsung Galaxy S23)
- **Operating system and version:** (e.g., iOS 17, Android 13)
- **Firebase Cloud Messaging (FCM) token:** Unique identifier for sending push notifications to your device
- **App version:** The version of Skate Monkey you're using

### 2.5 Usage Data
We collect information about how you use the App:
- **Game activity:** Games created, players invited, tricks generated, game results
- **Trick submissions:** Records of tricks you attempted and whether you landed or missed them
- **App interactions:** Features you use, screens you view, buttons you tap

### 2.6 User-Generated Content
- **Videos:** Skateboarding trick videos you record and upload
- **Messages:** Text messages you send to other players in your games
- **Video messages:** Videos you send as messages to other players. Videos selected from your device's gallery may include bystanders or other players. We do not perform automatic recognition to identify individuals or extract identifiers for that purpose, such as facial geometry, voiceprints, or gait signatures. We do not currently use uploaded images, audio, videos, or messages to train AI models or process that content with AI moderation tools.

### 2.7 Technical Data
- **IP address:** Your device's internet protocol address
- **Crash logs and error reports:** Technical information when the App crashes or encounters errors
- **Performance data:** App loading times and performance metrics

## 7. Data Retention

### 7.1 Active Accounts
We retain your information for as long as your account is active or as needed to provide you the Service.

### 7.2 Game Data and Content
Games and their associated content, including messages, are automatically deleted 30 days after a game finishes or after 90 days without activity. An unfinished game that other players keep active may remain beyond 90 days after a participant deletes their account. Account deletion removes that participant's uploaded videos as described in Section 7.3. This helps us keep the Service affordable and sustainable. **You should keep your original videos** as we do not guarantee indefinite storage of game content.

### 7.3 Account Deletion
When you delete your account, we immediately delete your login account, profile (including your name, username, date of birth, skill level, and footedness), profile photos, and all videos you uploaded from active systems. Your videos no longer appear in ongoing or completed games. Residual copies and retained records are described below.

Text messages you sent remain visible to the other players in each game under "Deleted User." We immediately replace references to your account in each game with a random identifier that is different for each game. This removes the account link and prevents those identifiers from linking your messages across games. The message text is not changed and may still identify you or someone else, for example through a social handle you included. We therefore describe these messages as no longer linked to your account, not as anonymized. Messages remain until the game is deleted under Section 7.2. You may ask support at skatemonkeydev@gmail.com to remove specific messages sooner.

Deleted photos and videos remain recoverable through Google Cloud Storage soft delete for seven days before permanent purge. We do not intend to create separate backups of photos or videos. Server logs are retained for 30 days.

The Firestore database is backed up daily and retained for 14-days. Once implemented, these backups will be used only for disaster recovery, not for analytics or routine access. These periods concern different data stores and do not mean that every item is erased from every system within 14 days.

To prevent abuse of the one-game free trial, we retain your email address and trial-use date after account deletion solely to enforce the trial limit, subject to Section 9.2 for under-13 users. We also retain reports filed against you for safety and enforcement of our Terms and Conditions. Information may also be retained as legally required under Section 7.4.

Google Analytics and Crashlytics data may remain after account deletion. We do not send your account ID to those services, and their data is not linked to your Skate Monkey account. This does not mean the data is necessarily anonymous. Google Analytics data is retained for no more than 14 months.

Account deletion does not cancel an App Store or Google Play subscription. You must cancel your subscription through the applicable store.

### 7.4 Legal Obligations
We may retain information longer if required by:
- Legal obligations (e.g., tax records, court orders)
- Ongoing investigations
- Dispute resolution

### 7.5 Anonymized Data
We may retain anonymized or aggregated data (data that cannot identify you) indefinitely for analytics and service improvement.

Text messages remain visible to other players as "Deleted User," no longer linked to your account, until the game is deleted as described in Section 7.2. Message text is not changed and may still contain identifying information. You may ask support to remove specific messages sooner. Deleting your account does not cancel an App Store or Google Play subscription. You must cancel your subscription through the applicable store.

### 8.4 Opt-Out of Push Notifications
You can disable push notifications at any time through:
- **iOS:** Settings > Notifications > Skate Monkey
- **Android:** Settings > Apps > Skate Monkey > Notifications

Note: Disabling notifications may impact your ability to stay updated on game activity.

### 8.5 Opt-Out of Marketing Communications
If we offer marketing communications in the future, you may opt out by:
- Clicking "unsubscribe" in the email

## 9. Children's Privacy (Ages 13+)

### 9.1 Age Requirement
Skate Monkey is intended for users ages 13 and older and is not directed to children under 13. Children under 13 may not create an account or use the App. Users must provide accurate information; a parent or guardian's permission does not make an under-13 user eligible to use the App. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

### 9.2 Compliance with COPPA
The Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) applies to services directed to children under 13 and to operators with actual knowledge that they collect personal information from children under 13. Our age restriction is not a substitute for applicable COPPA obligations. In particular:
- We require users to be at least 13 years old
- We do not knowingly collect information from children under 13
- If we learn that a user is under 13 or that we have collected personal information from a child under 13, we will stop the child's access, promptly investigate, and delete the child's personal information from active systems and direct our service providers to delete it, unless retention is legally required. We will not retain an under-13 child's email address for the free-trial limit described in Section 7.3. A parent or guardian may report an under-13 account or request deletion at skatemonkeydev@gmail.com. We may request information reasonably necessary to verify the request without collecting more information from the child than necessary.

### 9.3 Notice for Parents and Legal Guardians of Users Ages 13-17
**Parents and legal guardians of users ages 13-17 should be aware:**
- Minors may create accounts with permission from a parent or legal guardian
- Minors' usernames, display names, profile photos, videos, and messages will be visible to other players in their games
- Minors may participate in games with adult users (18+)
- Minors may send and receive messages from other players, including adults
- We do not actively monitor all communications
- Parents and legal guardians should discuss online safety and appropriate behavior with their children
- Parents and legal guardians are encouraged to supervise their children's use of the App

### 9.4 If You Believe a Child Under 13 Is Using the App
If you believe a child under 13 has created an account or submitted personal information through the App, please contact us at skatemonkeydev@gmail.com with the subject "Under-13 Privacy Request." We will investigate and take the steps described in Section 9.2. Please do not send the child's password, videos, or other sensitive information with your initial report.

## 12. California Privacy Rights (CCPA)

If you are a California resident, the rights described in this section apply to the extent Skate Monkey is subject to the California Consumer Privacy Act, as amended (CCPA). Availability of the App in California alone does not determine whether the CCPA applies. Regardless of coverage, you may contact us about the access, correction, and deletion options described in Section 8.

### 12.1 Right to Know
You have the right to request:
- Categories of personal information we collect
- Categories of sources from which we collect information
- Business or commercial purposes for collecting information
- Categories of third parties with whom we share information
- Specific pieces of personal information we have about you

### 12.2 Right to Correct and Delete
You may request correction of inaccurate personal information we maintain about you. You may also request deletion of personal information collected from you, subject to applicable exceptions, including certain legal, security, and transaction-related needs.

### 12.3 Sale, Sharing, Sensitive Information, and Automated Decisions
We do not sell personal information or share it for cross-context behavioral advertising, as those terms are defined under California law. Disclosures to providers for App operations, analytics, and crash reporting are described in Sections 5 and 10; ordinary operational disclosures are distinct from California-law "sharing." We do not currently have advertising.

We do not collect precise location or perform biometric identification. Account credentials and private message contents may constitute sensitive personal information under California law. We process private messages to deliver them and investigate reports, and account authentication information to provide account access. We do not make automated decisions with legal or similarly significant effects.

Where applicable law provides rights to opt out of sale or sharing, limit certain uses of sensitive information, or access or opt out of covered automated decisionmaking, those rights remain subject to the practices that trigger them. Our current no-sale/no-sharing practice applies equally to California and other users.

### 12.4 Right to Non-Discrimination
You have the right not to receive discriminatory treatment for exercising your CCPA rights. We will not:
- Deny you goods or services
- Charge different prices or rates
- Provide a different level of quality
- Suggest you will receive a different price or quality

### 12.5 How to Exercise Your Rights
To exercise your CCPA rights, contact us at:
- **Email:** skatemonkeydev@gmail.com
- **Subject Line:** "California Privacy Rights Request"

Include:
- Your full name
- Email address associated with your account
- Description of the information you're requesting

For verifiable requests subject to the CCPA, we will respond within 45 days after receipt. If reasonably necessary, we may extend that period once by up to 45 additional days and will notify you of the extension within the initial 45 days. You do not need to create a new account to submit a request. If we maintain a website or another required request channel, we will make that channel available as applicable.

### 12.6 Verification
To protect your privacy, we will use information reasonably necessary to verify requests to know, correct, or delete, considering the sensitivity of the information and the request. We will not require verification for an opt-out request beyond what applicable law permits. Depending on the request, we may ask you to:
- Confirm your email address
- Provide account information
- Provide information reasonably necessary to match the request to our records; do not send passwords or sensitive identification documents with your initial request

### 12.7 Authorized Agents
You may designate an authorized agent to make requests on your behalf. We may request proof of authorization and, where permitted, ask you to verify your identity directly. A parent or legal guardian may submit a request on behalf of a minor child, subject to appropriate verification.
